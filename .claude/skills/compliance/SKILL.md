# Compliance

Confirm every period below against the current scheme or regulator wording before
relying on it; rules change.

## Per job (check on every job at stage 09 and 10)

| Item | Usual deadline | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| MCS certificate | 10 working days from commissioning | Certificate in the handover pack |
| DNO notification (G98) or pre-approval (G99) | G98: 28 days after commissioning. G99: approval before install | Reference in the job diary |
| Building regulations (Part P) via NAPIT | 30 days from completion | Compliance certificate |
| Electrical Installation Certificate and test results | At commissioning | Signed EIC |
| Deposit and workmanship insurance (domestic) | Before work starts | Policy in the pack |
| Structural and fire considerations for the roof and battery location | Before install | Survey notes, photos |

For projects where we are principal contractor: construction phase plan, RAMS, asbestos
information, building control appointment and inspections, F10 where the job is
notifiable, signed contract before starting on site.

## Business

List each with its renewal date from Gmail, Drive or the calendar, and flag anything
due inside 60 days: MCS annual surveillance; NAPIT assessment (including the site
addresses they ask for in advance); public and employers' liability, professional
indemnity; test instrument calibration; consumer code membership; waste carrier
registration; vehicle MOT, tax and insurance; design and certification software; data
protection registration; Companies House confirmation statement and accounts; VAT,
PAYE and CIS filing dates.

## Output

A dated list: overdue, due in 30 days, due in 60 days. Propose calendar entries for
anything missing from the diary (create only on approval). For an audit, list the jobs
since the last assessment with whether each has a complete pack.
